Write for Us

Are you passionate about writing and have useful ideas to share? The Living Guide welcomes thoughtful, original contributions that can provide genuine value to our readers.

We are interested in practical, informative, and engaging articles across a variety of everyday topics.

Topics We Accept

We welcome well-written articles related to:

  • Food — recipes, cooking ideas, food guides, and helpful tips.
  • Health & Wellness — healthy habits, general wellness, and lifestyle-focused topics.
  • Beauty & Self-Care — skincare, beauty routines, and self-care ideas.
  • Travel — destinations, travel tips, planning guides, and experiences.
  • Lifestyle — everyday living, personal growth, home, habits, and well-being.
  • Tips & Guides — practical advice, how-to articles, and useful everyday information.

We may also consider other relevant topics that fit the interests of our readers.

Content Guidelines

To maintain the quality of The Living Guide, submitted articles should:

  • Be original and not previously published elsewhere.
  • Be between 800 and 1,000 words.
  • Provide useful, relevant, and meaningful information.
  • Be clear, well-structured, and easy to read.
  • Use appropriate headings and paragraphs.
  • Be written for readers rather than primarily for search engines.
  • Avoid misleading, offensive, or inappropriate content.
  • Be properly researched where factual claims require supporting information.
Word Count Requirement: All guest posts should be between 800–1,000 words. Submissions outside this range may be returned for revision or declined.

Originality Matters

We only consider original submissions. Content copied from other websites or submitted without the appropriate rights may not be accepted.

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions are their own work and do not violate the rights of others.

Links and Promotional Content

Articles should not be written primarily for advertising or backlink purposes.

Relevant links may be considered when they genuinely add value for readers. Promotional links, excessive self-promotion, or unnatural keyword-focused links may be removed or declined.

Submission Process

If you would like to contribute, please email your article or article proposal to:

Email: thelivingguide@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your proposed article title.
  • A brief description of the article.
  • Your name.
  • The completed article, if it is ready for submission.
  • Any relevant information about yourself or your website.

We will review submissions based on relevance, originality, quality, and suitability for our readers.

Editorial Rights

Submitting an article does not guarantee publication.

The Living Guide reserves the right to edit submissions for grammar, clarity, structure, accuracy, formatting, and editorial quality. We may also decline or remove content that does not meet our guidelines.

Publication decisions are made at the discretion of The Living Guide.

Get in Touch

If you have an article idea or would like to discuss a potential contribution, we'd be happy to hear from you.

Email: thelivingguide@gmail.com
