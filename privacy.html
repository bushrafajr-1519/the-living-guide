Privacy Policy

At The Living Guide, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you may provide while using our website.

The Living Guide may collect limited information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit feedback, suggest an article, or communicate with us through the website.

This information may include details such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

We may also receive certain technical information automatically when you visit the website, such as your browser type, device information, general location information, and pages you visit. This type of information is generally used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance.

How We Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used to:

  • Review feedback and suggestions.
  • Communicate with contributors or potential collaborators.
  • Improve the website, articles, and overall user experience.
  • Understand general website traffic and visitor behavior.
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the website.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the website without appropriate notice or, where required, consent.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Living Guide may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, and provide a better experience for visitors.

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember certain information and understand how visitors interact with their pages.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

The Living Guide may use third-party services in the future, such as analytics or advertising services, to understand website traffic, improve content, or support the operation of the website.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

When third-party services are used, their respective privacy policies should also be reviewed to understand how they handle information.

Advertising

The Living Guide may display advertisements in the future through third-party advertising services.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display relevant advertisements and measure advertising performance, subject to their own policies and applicable settings.

If advertising services are added to the website, this Privacy Policy may be updated to provide additional information about those services.

Our website may contain links to external websites, services, or resources that are not operated by The Living Guide.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party website they choose to visit.

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through The Living Guide. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

Children's Privacy

The Living Guide is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or providing services. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on how you interact with the website, you may have choices regarding cookies, communications, and certain information you provide.
